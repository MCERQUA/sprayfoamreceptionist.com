# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 20, 2026

Spray Foam Receptionist ("we," "us") operates sprayfoamreceptionist.com (the "Site") as an educational resource for spray foam insulation contractors. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Site and how we use it.

## Information We Collect

**Information you provide directly.** When you submit a form on this Site — including the Free Call Coverage Assessment request form or the general contact form — we collect the information you enter, which may include your name, phone number, company name, and any message or details you provide.

**Automatically collected information.** Like most websites, this Site may collect basic technical information automatically, such as your browser type, general location (derived from IP address), pages visited, and referring website — used to understand how the Site is used and to identify how visitors found us.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, schedule and conduct a call coverage assessment conversation if requested, and improve the resources on this Site. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## Form Submissions and Third-Party Services

Forms on this Site are processed through our website hosting provider's form-handling service. Submitted information is transmitted securely and used solely for the purposes described above.

## Data Retention

We retain information submitted through this Site for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain appropriate business records, consistent with applicable law.

## Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request corrections, or to request deletion, subject to any legal or legitimate business recordkeeping requirements.

## Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for business use by contractors and business owners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
