## What We Can Help With

Common questions we hear include whether a call-handling problem is even worth addressing for a smaller crew, how to think about the cost of a paid answering solution against what missed calls are actually costing, and general questions about how live-agent, AI, and text-back options compare. ## What We're Not the Right Contact For

We're an educational resource, not an answering-service provider — if you're looking to actually purchase or sign up for phone-answering coverage, this isn't the place to do that directly. We can help you think through what to look for and ask the right questions of a real provider, but the purchase itself would be with whichever provider you ultimately choose.
