# Topical Map — sprayfoamreceptionist.com ("Spray Foam Receptionist")

## Site classification
Trade-service-adjacent B2B lead-gen/educational resource (NOT insurance). Business concept: a
resource for spray foam insulation contractors on the cost of missed calls and how to fix
call-handling gaps — NOT a literal answering-service product CCA operates. Branded as its own
resource — "Spray Foam Receptionist" — NOT Contractors Choice Agency; CCA's canonical phone/
email/address are reused only as the contact channel for the assessment/consultation CTA.

## Framing decision (see keywords.json / competitors.md for full research)
The real "answering service for contractors" market carries extreme CPC ($100-330+) and is
dominated entirely by actual operating companies (live-agent call centers, AI voice platforms).
CCA does not run a call center. Building a literal product-purchase page with invented pricing
would be dishonest and could cause real harm. Instead: an educational buyer's-guide resource
specific to spray foam contractors' actual call-handling problems, ending in a soft, honest CTA
("Get Your Free Call Coverage Assessment" — a real conversation about their situation, not a
fake checkout flow).

## Pillar
**sprayfoamreceptionist.com/** — homepage. "Why Spray Foam Contractors Can't Afford to Miss a
Call" — the core problem (crews physically can't answer phones on job sites, jobs are
schedule/weather-sensitive, competitors answer faster and win the job), routes to all cluster
pages + the assessment request page.

## Cluster / guide pages (6)
1. **/cost-of-missed-calls/** — Quantifies what a missed call actually costs a spray foam
   contractor in lost job value. Targets "cost of a missed call."
2. **/answering-service-vs-ai-vs-voicemail/** — Buyer's-guide comparison of live-agent answering
   services, AI voice/chat answering, and plain voicemail — honest tradeoffs of each. Targets
   "live answering service," "answering service vs virtual assistant."
3. **/after-hours-and-weekend-calls/** — The specific problem of leads calling outside business
   hours while crews are off the clock. Targets "after hours answering service" (1,300/mo,
   $110.09 CPC — highest volume in the set).
4. **/missed-call-text-back/** — Missed-call-text-back automation as a lower-cost, lighter-weight
   partial solution than a full answering service. Targets "missed call text back service."
5. **/choosing-an-answering-service/** — A buyer's checklist: what to actually look for (industry
   familiarity, scheduling integration, pricing models, contract terms) before picking a
   provider. Targets "best answering service for small business" (880/mo, $142.87 CPC).
6. **/spray-foam-call-handling/** — What's genuinely different about handling calls for spray
   foam specifically (urgent same-day quote requests, weather/schedule sensitivity, warranty and
   callback questions) versus generic contractor call scripts. The core differentiation angle —
   no competitor narrows this far.
