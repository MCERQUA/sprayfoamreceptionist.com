CRITICAL FRAMING DECISION (this build's central judgment call): the real "answering service for
contractors" market carries extreme CPC ($100-330+, highest of any keyword set researched this
session) and is dominated entirely by actual operating companies — live-agent call centers
(AnswerPro, AnswerHero, Posh, ReceptionHQ, Smith.ai, MAP Communications) and AI voice-answering
startups (Dapta.ai, Whippy.ai, Bland.ai, Retell AI, Insighto.ai). CCA does not operate a call
center, does not have live agents, and does not have an AI answering platform. Building a literal
sales page with fake pricing tiers and a signup flow for a phone-answering product that doesn't
exist would be dishonest and could cause real harm (a contractor believing they're purchasing
live phone coverage that was never real) — exactly the failure mode the task explicitly warned
against. DECISION: built as an honest educational resource + buyer's guide, with a consultative
"Get Your Free Call Coverage Assessment" CTA framed explicitly as a real conversation, not a
purchase flow. Verified via grep that no fabricated pricing tiers, fake testimonials, "trusted by
X contractors" claims, or language implying CCA itself answers calls made it into the site —
every mention of market pricing is framed as general information about third-party providers.
Terms of Use explicitly states this Site is not an answering service or telecommunications
provider. This mirrors the successful non-insurance-clone pattern from
subcontractorindemnityagreement.com earlier this session, applied to a B2B SaaS/service category
instead of an insurance-adjacent one.
